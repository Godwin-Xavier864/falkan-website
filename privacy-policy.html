Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Owned & Operated by Mangozteen (Kerala, India)

At Falkan ("we," "our," or "the Platform"), owned and operated by Mangozteen, a company based in Kerala, India, we are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you visit our website, application, or simulated trading arena, and our practices for collecting, using, maintaining, protecting, and disclosing that data.

1. Nature of the Platform & Zero Financial Data

Falkan is strictly an educational paper trading simulation and AI research platform. We do not request, process, or store credit card numbers, bank account details, demat account credentials, or any real-money payment instruments. All capital displayed within the trading arena is virtual simulation credit with zero monetary value.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to deliver, enhance, and secure your simulation experience:

  • Account Registration Details: When you register on the Falkan app, we collect basic identifiers such as your username, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Simulation & Activity Data: Simulated orders placed, virtual portfolio holdings, leaderboard XP, and learning progress within the Falkan Academy.
  • Technical & Log Information: Standard browser type, IP address, device telemetry, access times, and referring URLs to optimize application speed, reliability, and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected information solely for operational and educational purposes:

  • To provision and administer your virtual paper-trading account and track portfolio metrics.
  • To personalize AI Research Engine outputs and relevant market educational materials.
  • To manage trader ranks, achievements, and leaderboard statistics.
  • To diagnose server performance issues and safeguard platform integrity against spam or abuse.

4. Data Protection & Security

Mangozteen employs industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and robust technical safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, accidental loss, alteration, or disclosure. Access to user records is restricted to authorized personnel who are subject to strict confidentiality obligations.

5. Information Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise monetize your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymous, aggregated statistical information regarding general trading patterns for research or educational publications, ensuring no individual user can be identified.

6. Cookies and Analytical Technologies

We may use minimal session cookies and local storage tokens solely to maintain your active session state, preserve your workspace layout preferences, and evaluate website traffic patterns. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though certain interactive features may experience reduced functionality.

7. User Rights and Data Retention

You have the right to request access to your personal information, request corrections, or ask for the deletion of your account and associated simulation history. To exercise any of these rights, you may reach out to Mangozteen's support team at the contact details provided below.
